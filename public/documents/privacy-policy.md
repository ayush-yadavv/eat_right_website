## 1. Personal Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us, information collected automatically when you use our digital properties, data from integrated device ecosystems, and information from trusted third parties.

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly
* **Contact & Identity Data:** Your name, email address, and optional telephone number provided during registration, onboarding, or customer support inquiries.
* **Account Credentials & Identifiers:** Unique user identifiers (such as your Firebase UID) and login credentials used to authenticate your account.
* **Profile & Demographic Data:** Biographical information including age, date of birth, biological sex or gender, height, current body weight, target weight, fitness goals, and general activity levels.
* **Dietary Preferences & Special Category Health Data:** Information you share regarding your nutritional goals, dietary preferences (e.g., vegetarian, keto, Mediterranean), food sensitivities, and allergen restrictions (e.g., gluten, nuts, dairy).
* **Meal Logs & User-Generated Content:** Meal records, food diary entries, timestamps, ingredient notes, fasting intervals, and photographs of food items uploaded for AI meal scanning, as well as associated image metadata (such as capture time).
* **Communications & Support Data:** Messages, feedback, inquiries, and transcripts of communications with our team via email or support channels.
* **AI Chat Queries & Prompts:** Questions, dietary requests, recipe queries, and inputs submitted to the Annura AI assistant.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically
When you access our Site or App, we and our service providers may automatically log information about your device and interaction:
* **Device Telemetry:** Operating system version, device model and manufacturer, device type (e.g., smartphone or tablet), system language, screen resolution, and time zone.
* **App Performance & Crash Diagnostics:** Anonymous diagnostic crash reports, error stack traces, and app performance metrics collected via Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring to diagnose bugs and maintain platform reliability.
* **Push Notification Tokens:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) registration tokens used solely to transmit requested meal, fasting, and hydration reminders.
* **Cookieless Web Telemetry (Website Only):** When you browse our informational website ([https://annura.ay7.me](https://annura.ay7.me)), we use **Vercel Web Analytics** to collect aggregate, anonymous page performance data. This service operates completely without cookies, does not use persistent local storage, does not track visitors across websites, and masks IP addresses at the regional level.
* **No Mobile Ad Tracking:** The Annura AI mobile application contains **zero** third-party advertising cookies, cross-app tracking identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID), or commercial marketing SDKs.

### 1.3 Third-Party Platform & Ecosystem Sources
* **Google Health Connect (Processed 100% Locally on Device):** With your explicit consent, Annura AI reads and syncs health and fitness metrics from your on-device Google Health Connect repository (such as Calories, Body Weight, Steps, Sleep, and Workouts). In strict accordance with the Google Play Health Connect Developer Policy, this data is **processed 100% locally on your device** and is never uploaded to or stored on our cloud servers.
* **System Voice Assistants & Shortcuts (App Actions / Google Assistant / Gemini):** Spoken intents and shortcut parameters (such as logging a meal or checking a fasting timer) are processed by your device's operating system and delivered locally to the App via deep links. Sensitive health conditions are never broadcast to ambient or public smart displays.

### 1.4 Information About Others
If you utilize referral or invite features to share Annura AI with colleagues or family, we use the recipient's contact details solely to deliver the invitation. You represent that you have received consent from that individual before providing their contact information.

## 2. Tracking, Artificial Intelligence (AI) & Device Technologies

### 2.1 Cookies and Cookieless Architecture
* **Website:** Our informational website does not utilize tracking cookies, advertising pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts. Telemetry is collected in aggregated form via cookieless Vercel Web Analytics. Because no non-essential cookies or personal identifiers are set, no intrusive cookie consent banner is required under the EU ePrivacy Directive or GDPR.
* **Mobile App:** The mobile application does not employ web tracking cookies or commercial ad trackers.

### 2.2 Generative Artificial Intelligence (AI) Models
Annura AI integrates state-of-the-art generative artificial intelligence models (including Google Gemini models through Google GenAI APIs) to deliver intelligent nutrition assistance, meal photo breakdowns, and personalized dietary advice:
* **Ephemeral Vision Analysis:** Food images captured for meal logging are analyzed ephemerally in-memory by Google Gemini vision models to estimate caloric density and macro splits. Images are not archived or permanently stored in cloud storage.
* **Enterprise Privacy Safeguards:** Data processed through Google GenAI APIs is governed by enterprise confidentiality commitments. Your queries, meal photographs, and personal health metrics are **never used to train, fine-tune, or improve public foundation AI models**.
* **Safety & Prompt Injection Protections:** Prompts and responses pass through automated safety guardrails to filter harmful, unsafe, or non-dietary content. Users may report erroneous or inappropriate AI responses using our in-app reporting tools.

### 2.3 Google Health Connect Compliance
Our integration with Google Health Connect adheres strictly to the [Google Play Health Connect Developer Policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13010189), including the **Limited Use** requirements:
1. **Limited Use:** Health Connect metrics are utilized solely to provide and improve in-app nutrition tracking and user-visible health dashboards.
2. **On-Device Isolation:** Health Connect data is processed entirely on your physical device. We do not transmit, sync, or retain Health Connect metrics on external servers.
3. **Strict Ban on Data Sale:** Health Connect data is **never** sold, transferred, licensed, or brokered to advertising platforms, data brokers, or information resellers.
4. **No Advertising or Profiling:** Consumer health metrics are never used to serve interest-based advertisements or create marketing profiles.

## 5. Data Retention Policy

We retain personal information only for the minimum period necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, satisfy legal and regulatory requirements, and resolve disputes.

### 5.1 Specific Retention Schedules by Data Category
* **Account Identity & Profile Credentials:** Retained for the duration of your active account. Permanently deleted from production databases upon account deletion.
* **Meal Logs & Nutritional Records:** Retained in Cloud Firestore while your account is active to provide longitudinal dietary tracking. Users can delete individual meal entries at any time directly in the app.
* **Food Scan Photos (Zero Cloud Storage):** Processed ephemerally in-memory by Google Gemini AI. Image files are not retained or archived in our cloud infrastructure.
* **Google Health Connect Data (Zero Cloud Retention):** Retained 0 seconds in our cloud infrastructure. All metrics are processed 100% locally on your device.
* **AI Chat Histories:** Stored with your user account in Cloud Firestore while your account remains active and purged immediately upon account termination.
* **Push Notification Device Tokens:** Retained while your device remains registered; deleted upon user sign-out, device deregistration, or app uninstallation.
* **Crash & Diagnostic Logs:** Anonymized logs in Firebase Crashlytics are permanently purged on a rolling **90-day** maximum retention schedule.
* **Rolling Backup Archives:** Encrypted disaster recovery database backups are overwritten and permanently purged within **30 days** of deletion.
