## 3. When Does MyFitnessPal Still Make Sense?

To remain completely objective and fair, there are specific scenarios where MyFitnessPal may still be the right fit:

1. **You depend on an obscure, community-entered restaurant food:** MyFitnessPal's 15+ years of crowdsourced submissions mean almost any local restaurant dish has been logged by someone (even if portion accuracy varies).
2. **You use niche fitness trackers with legacy integrations:** If you rely on specialized power meters or proprietary gym equipment that only connects to Under Armour's API ecosystem, MyFitnessPal offers established plumbing.
3. **You are strictly focused on CICO body composition without concern for food processing:** If you follow an "If It Fits Your Macros" (IIFYM) protocol and do not mind manual grams-and-ounces entry, MyFitnessPal's legacy interface will feel familiar.
